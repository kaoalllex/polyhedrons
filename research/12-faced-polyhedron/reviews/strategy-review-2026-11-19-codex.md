**PROVED-1, model comparison.** GP signs can describe an abstract OM. P3 constructs occupied topes and reads edge runs in their induced orders. **PROVED-1, safe map blocking.** Use a Boolean predicate for each complete run on line ij with endpoint triples ijk, ijl, equivalent in both directions to the decoded run. For free C2 the normaliser has order 2⁴·4! = 384 (swap partners, permute pairs). It only exhausts labellings for one marked action up to conjugacy. ## 3. Item 2: a credible census gate and a necessary geometric test

**PROVED-1, cost inputs.** At (9,4), E=45 and there are 30 dual triangles. The 36 unordered pairs form 12 free C3 orbits. Distributing nine extra edges means a multiset of three orbits: 364 demands, including repetitions. Each ij edge needs two distinct corner triples out of seven, hence multiplicity at most three. This removes 12 demands with multiplicity four. Session count: canonicalising the 352 remaining triples of pair orbits under permutations of the three cycles, independent cyclic shifts, and simultaneous reversal gives 18 representatives. Generate each pair orbit by taking the least unordered image under the three rotations, then take the least sorted demand under those relabellings. (STATUS.md:159–162, c3_crosscheck.py:19–37,78–95.)

A fixed triangle uses an entire face-label orbit. There are six *oriented* fixed rows but only three distinct supports. Since 30−b is divisible by three, b=0 or 3 survives admissibility. Six does not. Reject opposite orientations of the same support, including moving rows. Pair equivariantly on one representative per unordered pair orbit and transport the matching. The old code constructs a Cartesian product over all pairs before filtering commutation, hard-codes 26 triangles and genus three, and checks the deadline outside gluing. C2 needs special treatment when the involution reverses an unordered edge, and its fixed face must have a single link. (c3_crosscheck.py:40–71,92–118.)

**PROVED-1, fixed-face C2.** Centre the half-turn on its axis. Its fixed outward plane is horizontal. A swapped pair and that plane have dependent normals, accounting for the standard-INF failure. ## 4. Audit of the symmetry lemmas

**PROVED-1, signed stabilisers.** Reran reviews/strategy-review-2026-11-19-orbits.py:28–48,56–70. It returns D2/S4/free C2/C3 orbit counts 36/34/66/72, zero forced quads for free C2 at eight, four with INF and zero plane-only for fixed-face C2 at nine. Every tested mirror partition has a forced zero. For outward coorientations, transport factors are positive. A setwise stabiliser forces zero exactly when det(g) times its permutation sign on the quadruple is negative. Test *every group element*, including powers; omit INF in a moved chart. Arbitrarily signed plane representatives require their orientation factors too. This is the exact stabiliser obstruction, not a test of all geometric dependencies. A proper five-cycle can force rank loss without fixing any four of its five planes. (p3sym.py:3–10,37–57.)

**PROVED-1, owner 5 and I-261118-02.** A fixed face keeps its outward normal. A nonidentity rotation fixes at most one face since two distinct perpendicular support planes would be parallel and could not share an edge. A prime-order rotation p≥5 has a moving orbit of covectors lying in dimension at most three: the horizontal normal rotates in two dimensions and (vertical normal, offset) stays constant. This includes concurrency at infinity, even when the orbit planes miss the affine axis. Thus no such orbit is uniform. A nontrivial finite point group contains an element of prime order, even if neither 2 nor 3 divides its order. Odd-order elements are proper. At eight an order-three action would need at least two fixed faces, and a proper involution must be free. At nine a proper involution fixes one face and an order-three rotation is free. (I-261118-02:12–19, lead review:61–67.)

Improper involutions are reflection or inversion. Inversion pairs parallel planes and fixes no outward face. A mirror swapping two pairs forces a plane quadruple zero; with fewer swapped pairs at these sizes four fixed covectors already lie in a 3-space. A genuine rotoreflection fixes no outward normal. Its square is proper, so its presence need not supply a mirror: the order-four eight-face example supplies a half-turn through its square. At nine, D2 and cyclic order four of improper type cannot act: their orbits have even size unless there is a globally fixed outward normal, which neither group has. A larger group containing C3 can be searched retaining C3 alone. (p3sym.py:31–34, I-261118-02:31–36.)

**PROVED-1, lead 4.** On the twenty Aut+=2 maps the square of a rotoreflection of order greater than two must induce the unique proper involution (the face action is faithful since the normals span space). It commutes with its square, hence fixes the square's unique fixed face, impossible. On an Aut+=1 map its square must be identity; Aut+=3 cannot supply a proper order-three isometry on eleven faces (11 mod 3 = 2). Odd face count also rules out inversion. This supports closing uniform improper cases for these census classes. Mirror-symmetric nonuniform embeddings and asymmetric realizations require different coverage. (STATUS.md:421–430, E-261118-03/CONCLUSION.md:23–25,36–41.)

**PROVED-1, perturbation step.** For the retained subgroup, parameterise each moving orbit by one free covector, the C2 fixed plane by (0,0,c,d), and the new infinity plane in an open far-chart region. All forbidden minors are polynomials. Direct integer checks: for C3 cyclically permute xyz on (2,3,5,7), (1,−4,2,11), (5,−1,6,13), add standard INF; minimum absolute minor over 210 quads is 8. For C2 use representatives (2,3,5,7), (1,−4,3,11), (5,−1,6,13), (3,7,−2,17), negate x,y on partners, add (0,0,1,19) and chart covector (2,5,7,100000); minimum is 44. Hence the product polynomial is nonzero and its complement is dense in this parameter space. (Conventions: code/omsat.py:47–69, E-261110-04/PLAN.md:24–26.)

Application to a solid also needs a relatively open neighbourhood preserving its map: nonsingular incident triples move continuously, strict endpoint orders survive, disjoint compact features have positive clearance, and incident corners retain their local sectors. This argument is for proper cubic embedded disks with strict local incidences. It may break extra symmetries outside the retained subgroup; forced plane-only zeros cannot be cured by changing chart. The integer checks establish genericity, not openness at a pinched or noncubic configuration or a symmetric realization of an abstract OM. (SPEC.md:8–24,47–53, E-261117-02/CONCLUSION.md:50–57.)

## 5. Missing routes and the eight-face alternative

At each fixed cubic vertex the three edge rays are permuted cyclically. Its spherical link separates the two axial poles (rotation acts freely off the poles, and an invariant complementary disk must contain a fixed point). The line therefore crosses the boundary, so the number of fixed vertices is even. Together with b=0 or 3 this predicts b=0 for a geometric embedding. Cheapest test: classify fixed cells and signs on the eight existing (9,3) codes and a C3 tetrahedral control before considering a geometric filter. Retain b=3 in the *abstract census*.
