## Idea
After a chirotope is found, test whether its rational realization is reachable by numerical continuation from a random start; failure suggests a singular component.

## Why it might help
Universality means a SAT chirotope may still have an empty or singular realization space.

## Cheapest test
Continuation from a random start after a chirotope is found.
