## Idea
Minimal-surface view: our surfaces are mostly saddles (27-33 of 44), so the Gauss map is near an anti-holomorphic 5-sheeted branched cover (20 branch points, Riemann-Hurwitz genus 6); the period problem equals our 36 handle-period conditions; discrete minimal surfaces with planar faces exist (Bobenko-Hoffmann-Springborn).

## Why it might help
Borrows how the period problem is solved for genus-g minimal surfaces (symmetry or parameter continuity).

## Cheapest test
Build admissible normals from a degree-5 branched-cover model.
