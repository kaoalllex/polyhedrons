## Idea
12 planes = 3 orbits of a free group of order 4 (D2 or C4 rotoreflection) or 2 orbits of order 6; construct the best solid from the planes with the exact inner step.

## Why it might help
Their method rediscovered Szilassi and Mizhaev; 12 parameters instead of 48.

## Cheapest test
Rediscover RV's polyhedron from random D2 two-orbit planes (engine calibration), then n = 12, d in {0, 6, 12}.
