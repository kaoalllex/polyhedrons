## Idea
12 faces = orbits of a free group of order 4 or 6; enumerate base maps with voltages and derive face-neighbourly cubic maps with collinear doubled pairs (genus 7 or 8) for D2, C4, Z6.

## Why it might help
The combinatorial generator that relaxed targets L1/L2 need.

## Cheapest test
Generate and count all such maps; feed each into the symmetric plane search.
