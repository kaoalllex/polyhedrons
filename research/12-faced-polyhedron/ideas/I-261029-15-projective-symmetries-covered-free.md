## Idea
A finite group of projective maps preserving the bounded polyhedron P preserves conv(P); averaging in the dual cone gives an invariant hyperplane missing conv(P), so the action is affine, then isometric. Hence the symmetric non-existence theorem extends to projective symmetries.

Strengthens the theorem at no cost.
