## Idea

Search S6 at (10, 3) among symmetric realizations first. Screening of the 20 imported maps
(`reviews/strategy-review-2026-11-13-symmetry.py`; EXACT computation, no solver): 12 maps have orientation-preserving
automorphisms that pass two necessary conditions of a rotation of an embedded polyhedron (PROVED-1, one AI session,
not reviewed): the axis meets the surface in an even number of points (fixed faces, edges, vertices), and two fixed
faces are parallel, hence non-adjacent.

* C3 with one fixed face and one axis vertex: Lutz #1, 2, 4, 8, 14, 15. Free C2 (four axis edges): #6, 12, 20.
  Neither forces a zero quadruple (an invariant 4-set is permuted evenly), so the uniform symmetric option of
  `omsat.build` applies: ten formulas.
* C2 with two fixed faces: #15, 16, 17, 19, 20; needs a nonuniform encoder or arrangements (I-261113-04).
* Excluded: C7 on #8 (three fixed points), C4 on #17 (its square fixes two faces that it swaps).
* Lutz #15 has the rotation group T of the tetrahedron; its three involutions fix exactly the three non-adjacent
  pairs. The planes are forced up to one real parameter: `|x| = |y| = |z| = 1` and four planes `n_i . p = t`. Four
  planes meet at forced points (for example `(1, -1, t)`), so the arrangement is not simple.

## Why / cheapest test

The S0 symmetric formulas were decided where the asymmetric ones timed out (FD5, FD6); all 20 asymmetric (10, 3)
formulas timed out at 3600 s (STATUS 3af). Cheapest test: E-261113-01, the tetrahedral family by exact arrangement
cells, then the ten formulas. Untested proposal; the screening is necessary conditions only, not a realization.
