## Idea
At each degenerate limit (edge shrinking to 0, nearly concurrent planes) list the dual 2-2 flips and continue in the flipped map; figure-eight faces can only be repaired this way.

## Why it might help
Degenerate attractors are flip points that map-fixed searches treat as dead ends.

## Cheapest test
Tested on the degenerate genus-4 attempt: both flips create a doubled adjacency (1-6, 1-3), not a valid branch.
