## Idea

A 12-face polyhedron with 11-gonal faces and trivalent vertices is an equivelar polyhedral 2-manifold of type
{11,3} in E^3. That class has its own literature, never cited in this project: McMullen, Schulz and Wills (1982-83)
on equivelar polyhedral manifolds in E^3 and polyhedra of unusually large genus; Brehm and Wills (1993) survey of
polyhedral manifolds; Schulte and Wills (1985) on the polyhedral realization of Klein's map {3,7}_8 (whose dual is a
{7,3} with planar heptagons). Extract: every realized {p,3} with p >= 7 and planar faces (positive controls, and
the construction method); every theorem restricting {p,3} polyhedra with planar faces in E^3.

## Why it might help

A realized {7,3} or {8,3} with planar faces would be the closest known object to Szilassi-12 and a calibration case
for every engine; a published obstruction on {p,3} would change the plan at once.

## Cheapest test

One literature session on the three sources, primary texts only, with exact statements and page numbers.
