## Idea
Huang-style single certificate: one Farkas combination of the 36 period conditions, symmetric under Aut, valid for all admissible normals.

## Why it might help
Short impossibility proofs are often one dual certificate or matrix.

## Cheapest test
LP over certificates for many normal sets at once.
