## Idea
Prefer tube attachments with an end on an earlier tube ('one tunnel splitting in two') when straight attachments fail.

## Why it might help
Branching handles are an unexplored discrete choice.

## Cheapest test
Order beam-ladder candidates by whether a tube end lies on an earlier tube.
