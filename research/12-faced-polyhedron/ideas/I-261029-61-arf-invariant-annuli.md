## Idea

For any polyhedral immersion found, compute q(gamma_XYZ) = linking number of the boundary of F_X u F_Y u F_Z mod 2 and Arf(q).

## Why it might help

Arf = 1 means the regular-homotopy class of that immersion contains no embedding: an embedding-level invariant from three-face data.

## Cheapest test

Needs an immersion first.
