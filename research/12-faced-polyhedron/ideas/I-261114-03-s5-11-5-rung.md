## Idea

Maximal face-neighbourly cubic polyhedra with 11 faces have genus 5 and two doubled pairs (as at (8, 2)). Enumerate
the candidate maps with the E-261103-01 enumerator, then solve three normalised formulas at 12 elements.

## Why / cheapest test

If the normalised (10, 4) formulas are decided in one night, (11, 5) is the only S5 case left below S0, and its
formulas have the element count of S0. Cheapest test: one session (census size, three formula times). Kill: above
about 10^4 admissible maps or hours per formula. Only after E-261110-06 has run. Untested proposal.
