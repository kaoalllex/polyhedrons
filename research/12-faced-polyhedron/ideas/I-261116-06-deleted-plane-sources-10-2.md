## Idea

The realized (10, 2) S6 objects (paper outline C1, C2) are the only exact arrangements we own with a 10-face genus-2
solid on 36 adjacent pairs. Delete two of their planes to get 8-plane arrangements (a rung with known answers: the
(8, 3) objects exist) and one plane to get 9-plane arrangements; ask the inner solver of E-261112-01 for an all-pairs
solid with the genus free.

## Why it might help

A family independent of the two (8, 3) sources behind ninth-plane Stage A and E-261113-03, built from arrangements
that already carry a rich face-neighbourly solid. Untested proposal, not evidence.

## Cheapest test

The 8-plane arm of E-261110-03 (minutes): rate p of all-pairs solids; >= 10 positives earns the 9-plane arm.
