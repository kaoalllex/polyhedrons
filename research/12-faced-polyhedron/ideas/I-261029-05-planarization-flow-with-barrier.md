## Idea
Start from a smooth embedded genus-6 surface with the map drawn (e.g. Lawson-type) and deform toward planar faces with a barrier (Ricci flow / convex integration analogy), Surface-Evolver style.

## Why it might help
Deform inside the right class instead of searching.

## Cheapest test
Surface-Evolver-style local/global projection.
