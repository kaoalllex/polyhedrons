## Idea
Enumerate or sample integer orbit representatives with coefficients in [-6, 6] within the symmetric ansatz; exact arithmetic for free.

## Why it might help
RV planes have coefficients <= 5.

## Cheapest test
Fast filter (Gauss gate of the resulting map, pair count); hits with >= 60 pairs.
