## Idea
Certify non-realizability of a chirotope by a biquadratic final polynomial (Bokowski-Sturmfels, Richter-Gebert); only a partial method.

## Why it might help
Alternative certificate if SAT is inconclusive.

## Cheapest test
Take a chirotope UNSAT on a restricted sub-instance and search for a final polynomial with CAS.
