## Idea
Compare Mizhaev's, Roest-Vigh's and our n = 8 ladder maps (with doubled adjacencies) by map canonical form.

## Why it might help
Validates canonical forms on doubled maps before claiming any relaxed object new.

## Cheapest test
Reproduce 'not combinatorially equivalent' from the papers' data.

## Evidence (2026-11-03)

The dual maps of both objects were built from the published planes and face cycles and canonicalised (strategy review
2026-11-03, `reviews/strategy-review-2026-11-03-flipwalk.py` mode `control83`): distinct, |Aut+| = 8 and chiral (Roest-Vigh,
the dihedral group of order 8 of their Theorem 1) and |Aut+| = 2 with an orientation-reversing automorphism (Mizhaev, C4
generated by a rotoreflection), both with 8 doubled pairs and all faces 9-gons. The same canonical form (BFS code over all
roots and both orientations) serves the S5 census E-261103-01.
