## Idea
Four-colour-style discharging: sum_X w_X = 12 - s (fresh-eyes identity) with face-local bounds on the weights w_X and on reflex corners.

## Why it might help
Our obstruction is local, so local rules plus a global sum could give a contradiction.

## Cheapest test
Bound w_X from the geometry of one 11-gon and check the sum.
