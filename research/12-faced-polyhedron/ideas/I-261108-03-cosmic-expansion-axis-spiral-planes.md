## Idea

Look for a polyhedron shape in the structure of the expanding universe: planes diverge from a centre, possibly in a
spiral, around a central axis that is the main hole; further out, other hole-axes run around the main one.

## Why it might help

It names a concrete ansatz: one distinguished axis with the face planes arranged around it by a rotation or a screw
motion, the remaining handles placed around that axis.

## Why it is parked

* The physical analogy carries no structure. Cosmic expansion is homogeneous and isotropic, with no centre or axis. The
  cosmic web (walls, filaments, voids) is close to a convex-cell (Voronoi-type) tessellation, the opposite of a
  face-neighbourly surface with handles.
* An exact screw motion with non-zero translation is not a symmetry of a bounded object, so a spiral can only be an
  approximate seed pattern.
* An exact rotational axis is excluded for the 12-face problem: FD6 (no polyhedral immersion with a non-trivial
  rotational symmetry, EXACT for rotations). "One central hole, five around it" would be a C5 rotation, covered there.
  Mirrors and point reflections rest on I-261101-16 (PROVED-1, pending review).
* For the focus target (9, 3), all 8 uniform free-C3 formulas are UNSAT-VERIFIED (EXACT, those formulas only;
  `STATUS.md` section 3d-3i).
* Screw-orbit plane sets were tried (I-261029-04, DR16): LOW-YIELD@minutes each. That is a small budget, not a proof
  that the method is dead.

## What is not covered

Approximately spiral, non-symmetric starting arrangements for numerical search were not run as such. The current
pipeline is exact per map (DR19), where seeds matter little; a new run would need a PLAN.md that says how it differs
from DR16.
