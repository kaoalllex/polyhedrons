## Idea

For a Z9 Cayley map (I-261117-01) the fully symmetric ansatz has no continuous parameter: nine planes
(cos a t, sin a t, cos b t, sin b t), t = 2 pi k / 9, two classes (a, b) = (1, 2), (1, 3) (PROVED-1 sketch, lead);
only the chart varies. It differs from the moment-curve arrangement of the 2026-11-10 dialogue (another curve, all
charts). Geometric one-orbit C_n families are cones (A7), so the symmetry here is projective, acting through the chart.
