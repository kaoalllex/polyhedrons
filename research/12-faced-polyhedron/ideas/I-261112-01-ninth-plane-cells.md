## Idea

Replace random ninth planes and fixed E = 39 by distinct generic open chirotope cells of the ninth plane over
one of the two fixed eight-plane sources, including the plane at infinity in the sign data. Solve one exact
rational representative per symmetry orbit, requiring every pair adjacent and edge runs >= 39, genus free.
Stage A is the union of near-copy cells of the eight source planes; Stage B covers all cells, Roest-Vigh first.

## Why / cheapest test

The finite parameter space can be counted independently by Zaslavsky's formula; distinct exact representatives
plus an equal independently derived count gate coverage. Symmetry reduction needs exact set preservation and
actual orbit sizes. Near-copy lists have session checks only, not registered evidence. Stage A first tests the
list and the representative-to-solver path, including recovery after deleting and reinserting a source plane.
