## Statement

The Gauss class (degree, covering, saddles) is the main barrier above genus 3. Restated 2026-11-01 (strategy
review): at a fixed plane set, no handle can be attached to an embedded genus-3 polyhedron because the six handle
normals of an in-class handle must leave every open hemisphere, and that is incompatible with the embedding of the
base object. The sharper form is H-261101-10.

## Rival

It is necessary but not the binding constraint.

## Evidence so far

Explains the type-0 plateau (k_tau = 0 on 21/21 arms, and in 40 projective charts of one witness: not a chart
artefact, strategy review 2026-11-01 A.1); Q1 shows in-class normals can still fail (E-261030-09). The "pinned
hemisphere margin" of nights 4-5 is void: E-261101-07 found it to be a floor of the measuring function.

## What would move it

E-261101-19 (barrier continuation with a non-saturating measure); E-261101-06 (rank-3 SAT with Gauss clauses);
E-261101-16 (a higher-genus solid on an archived arrangement would refute it).
