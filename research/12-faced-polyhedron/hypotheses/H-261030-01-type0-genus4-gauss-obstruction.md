## Statement

The type-0 genus-4 plateau is a Gauss-class obstruction: every next handle of every type-0 genus-3 realization has k_tau = 0.

## Rival

The plateau is an optimizer weakness.

## Evidence so far

Exact on all 21 arms of the type-0 genus-3 realizations (k_tau = 0, degree -2 instead of -3; no admissible gauge after 1000-direction confirmation).

## What would move it

A type-0 genus-3 realization with an arm of k_tau = -1 (k-drift reached the class only on its wall, margin 0).
