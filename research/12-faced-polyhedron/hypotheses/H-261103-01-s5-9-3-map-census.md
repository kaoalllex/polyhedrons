## Statement

The dual maps of S5 candidates at (9, 3) (oriented triangular maps, 9 vertices, 39 edges, loopless, underlying simple
graph K9) form exactly 240 isomorphism classes (mirror images identified); 203 of them have all corner triples distinct
(the necessary condition for a geometric realization); 8 of the 203 have an order-3 automorphism acting freely on faces and
edges with exactly two fixed polyhedron vertices (2 of them with |Aut+| = 6), 24 have an involution, none is reflexible.
At (8, 2) there are exactly 2 classes, both admissible, both with a half-turn automorphism and no orientation-reversing one.

## Rival

The random walk missed classes (the counts are lower bounds only), or the sampler's canonical form or surface check has a
bug (then the counts are wrong in either direction).

## Evidence so far

Strategy review of 2026-11-03 (`reviews/strategy-review-2026-11-03.md` section 2.1, Appendix A): six independent flip
walks at (9, 3) (five canonical-polygon starts of 90-240 s and one Roest-Vigh-derived start) saturate at the same 240 codes;
every class seen at least 9 times; 99 % of classes within 27 s of a 240-s run. Controls: (7, 1) gives 1 class with
|Aut+| = 42 (correct), the 59 K12 types canonicalise to 59 codes with the FD3 automorphism orders, the two 8-face dual maps
are built and checked from the published data. CONJECTURE for completeness (sampling, not enumeration); the sampled maps
are in `reviews/strategy-review-2026-11-03-maps.json`.
