## Statement

Some 12-plane arrangement invariant under a group acting freely on the planes, or one of the 247 archived
arrangements, carries an all-adjacent cubic solid with at least 54 adjacent pairs (genus >= 4); for genus 6 that
solid is necessarily asymmetric (FD5).

## Rival

The maximal adjacency on every such arrangement is 48 or less, as on random arrangements (DR7).

## Evidence so far

None either way. FD5 excludes symmetric solids only. The 8-face objects of 2026 live on symmetric two-orbit
arrangements.

## What would move it

E-261101-16: a solid with >= 54 pairs supports it (and is an S3 witness of genus >= 4, the first); 48 on every
arrangement of the catalogue weakens it with a power statement (number and spread of arrangements decided).
