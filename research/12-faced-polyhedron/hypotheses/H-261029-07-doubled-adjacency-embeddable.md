## Statement

A pair of faces sharing two edges (doubled adjacency) can occur in an embedded polyhedron.

## Rival

Doubled adjacencies are never embeddable.

## Evidence so far

Settled: Mizhaev's and Roest-Vigh's 8-face polyhedra have 8 doubled pairs each and pass our exact verifier (EXACT; `code/control_rost_vigh.py`).
