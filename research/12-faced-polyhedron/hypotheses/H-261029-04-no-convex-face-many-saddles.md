## Statement

In any realization no face is convex and the saddle count is well above the bound 13.

## Rival

Realizations can have convex faces or about 13 saddles.

## Evidence so far

Admissible normals have 27-33 saddles of 44 (sprint board Q14); our genus-3 realizations were not measured.

## What would move it

Record convex faces and saddles on every exact realization in the archive (minutes).
