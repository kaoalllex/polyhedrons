## Statement

The Gauss-degree condition is rare: under 1e-3 of random normal sets per type pass it.

## Rival

Admissible normal sets are common, so the condition filters little.

## Evidence so far

Admissible normals are found by annealing in seconds (types 0, 5, 58), which shows they exist, not how frequent they are. Never measured on random samples.

## What would move it

Sample random normal sets per type and count the exact gate passes (minutes per type).
