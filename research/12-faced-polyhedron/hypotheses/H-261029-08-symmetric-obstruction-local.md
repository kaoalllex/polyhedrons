## Statement

For symmetric cases the obstruction is local: immersion clauses alone (no global triples) are already UNSAT.

## Rival

Global intersection conditions are needed.

## Evidence so far

Session tests #54 T, #3 D3, #45 C3 UNSAT without global clauses; then all 25 classes UNSAT with DRAT proofs (FD6).

## What would move it
