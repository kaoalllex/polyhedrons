## Statement

No polyhedral immersion (simple planar faces, embedded vertex stars) of any of the 59 maps is invariant under an
improper isometry. Together with the 25 certified rotation classes (FD6) this gives FD6 as stated.

## Rival

A mirror-symmetric immersion exists (its automorphism is one of the 21 orientation-preserving involutions).
