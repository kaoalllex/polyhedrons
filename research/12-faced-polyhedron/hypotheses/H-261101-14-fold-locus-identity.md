## Statement

The PL Gauss map satisfies a Quine-type identity relating the degree, the Euler characteristics of the convex
and saddle regions and vertex corrections, and this identity yields constraints on (number of convex vertices,
fold-locus length, convex runs per face) that are not implied by F3, F4, F6 and the face-winding identity.

## Rival

Every such constraint is already implied by the local identities.

## Evidence so far

None; the identity is conjectured from the smooth case.

## What would move it

E-261101-21: compute the quantities on the 247 archived realizations and the controls, find the identity, test
implication by adding it to the rank-3 encoding (E-261101-06) and counting models with and without it.
