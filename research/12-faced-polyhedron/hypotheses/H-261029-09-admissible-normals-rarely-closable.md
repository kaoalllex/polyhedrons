## Statement

Gauss-admissible normals are almost never closable: the realization space projects to a tiny set inside the admissible region.

## Rival

A positive fraction of admissible normals near a realization is closable.

## Evidence so far

Positive control: around a realized map the offsets are SAT only at r = 0.05 (one trial), UNSAT at r >= 0.1. 12,202 UNSAT at admissible normals (low power).

## What would move it

The control power study (planned experiment on normals-first power).
