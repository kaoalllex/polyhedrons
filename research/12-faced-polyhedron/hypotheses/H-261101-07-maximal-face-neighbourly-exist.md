## Statement

Maximal face-neighbourly cubic polyhedra with few doubled pairs exist for genus 2-5.

## Rival

They stop below genus 6.

## Evidence so far

8-face genus-3 objects exist (Mizhaev, Roest-Vigh); the maximal cases (8,2), (9,3), (10,4), (11,5) are untested.

## What would move it

Literature check, then the smallest case (8,2).
