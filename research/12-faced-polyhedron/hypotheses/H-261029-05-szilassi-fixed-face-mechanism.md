## Statement

Szilassi embeds because of its symmetry-fixed central convex face; a 12-face analogue has no fixed face.

## Rival

The fixed face is incidental.

## Evidence so far

Szilassi has exactly one convex face, the C2-fixed one; its solid is 8 chambers (Track 6). No 12-face map has a fixed face (FD5 context).

## What would move it

Look for an embedded n = 7 polyhedron without a convex face, or explain why one is forced.
