## Statement

Operational. Within one symmetry family at the 8-plane rung, arrangements with four disjoint plane pairs at an acute
plane angle <= 25 degrees contain an all-pairs solid (28 pairs adjacent, E free) at least twice as often as the other
arrangements of that family.

## Rival

No dependence once the family is fixed: the null model of the 2026-11-10 session already explains the matchings seen
in Szilassi, Roest-Vigh and Mizhaev (`reviews/HANDOFF_PROMPT_2026-11-10-wedges.md` §2).

## Evidence so far

None beyond that observation (NUMERICAL, weak, script not in the repository). E-261103-05 found one solid in its
first five random D2 arrangements; that is a stop at the first positive, not a rate.

## What would move it

E-261110-03: ratio >= 2 inside a family with >= 10 positives in total -> supported, and wedge seeding is used in
E-261110-04; ratio < 2 inside every family with >= 10 positives -> weakened, I-261110-01 parked with its rate; fewer
than 10 positives in total -> UNDERPOWERED, no move. Every later witness reports its wedge covariate (E-261110-01).
