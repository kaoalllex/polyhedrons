## Statement

Fixed-normal closure failures are cohomological: without the period conditions the LP is SAT, with them UNSAT.

## Rival

Local conditions alone already fail.

## Evidence so far

About 200 admissible normal sets behave so (FD9, NUMERICAL; `code/fresh_eyes/`).

## What would move it

Run `penrose_lp.py` on more than 100 further admissible sets; a single local failure weakens it.
